About ORA

Precision, elegance, and reliability in every tick.

ORA brings together a selection of world-class watch brands, offering luxury and everyday styles for every wrist. Our mission is to deliver premium timepieces with exceptional service, ensuring every purchase is memorable and meaningful.

Based in Kathmandu, we partner with trusted suppliers and maintain strict quality standards. Whether you’re choosing your first watch or expanding a collection, our team is passionate about helping you find the perfect match.

We believe a watch is more than an accessory—it’s a statement. From automatic movements to minimalist quartz designs, every piece in our catalog is curated for authenticity, durability, and timeless design.
